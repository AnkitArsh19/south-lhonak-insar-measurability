## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

This ledger documents a rigorous, unsparing peer audit of Phase 1 (Foundation, Exploratory Pilot InSAR, and Optical Baseline). In strict compliance with [`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md) (Rule #1: Scientific claims must have sources; Rule #5: Never silently discard results; Rule #6: Separation of inference levels), every discrepancy between preliminary exploratory drafts and physical ground truth is formally recorded with mathematical and physical verification.
